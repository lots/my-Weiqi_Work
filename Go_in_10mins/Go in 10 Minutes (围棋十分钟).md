# History (历史)
* The game of **Go** or **Weiqi**(围棋) was invented in China a few thousands year ago (or **Baduk** in Korean) (ref: [[weiqi_chronicle_v5c.pdf|History]]).  围棋在中国被发明已经是好几千年了.

# Objective (目的)
- To get more "**Points**" from the board than your opponent.  在棋盘上争夺比对手更多的"点"数.
# How to obtain "Points" (如何获得点数)
- Three methods (三种方法):
	1. Direct occupy (直接占据)
	2. Indirect occupy (间接围空)
	3. Captured stone (吃子/提子/俘虏敌子)

# Qi (Liberty) of Stones (棋的气)
- Concept: 气 (Qi or Liberty) and 一串子(String of stones)
# Enclosed Space (围住的空)
- The spaces surrounded by a single color of stones.  用同一种颜色围住的空地.
# How to reduce opponent's "Points" (如何减少对手的"点")

# How to end a game (如何结束一盘棋)
- Game can end at any time.  任何时候都可以结束棋局  
- Consecutive "Pass" from two players to signal the end of a game.  双方棋手连续各"虚手"一次表示棋局结束.
- Start to count "Points".  开始计"点".
#### Chinese counting (数子法)
- Total points = Direct occupy points + Indirect occupy points
#### Japanese counting (数目法)
- Total points = Indirect occupy points + Captured stones
